Gachagua To Ruto: Bring Back Our Lost Souls From The Russia-Ukraine Mess

The former Deputy President is finally waking up, and not a moment too soon. He's telling President Ruto, who's busy hobnobbing with Putin, to bring home the Kenyans who've been duped into fighting in Ukraine. Apparently, some bright sparks were promised jobs, but ended up on the front lines. Typical.

It seems our young men and women, desperate for a payday, have been lured to Russia with promises of civilian work, only to be handed military contracts and sent to fight a war they have no stake in. Gachagua is now demanding answers about those captured, those missing, and those who've simply vanished. You'd think our government would have this sorted by now, but then again, this is Kenya. Remember how our sovereignty became a side hustle? This whole passport for warlords situation feels eerily familiar. [Passports for Warlords: How Our Sovereignty Became a Side Hustle](/posts/passports-for-warlords--how-our-sovereignty-became-a-side-hustle)

The numbers are all over the place, as usual. Mudavadi says 354, while others are talking about nearly a thousand. It's a confused mess, with families left in the dark, wondering if their loved ones are dead, injured, or worse, prisoners. This recruitment scandal reeks of something rotten, with intermediaries pocketing cash while our youth become cannon fodder.

There was a deal in March 2026 to stop Kenyans from joining the Russian military. Clearly, that hasn't stopped the bleeding. Gachagua's plea is just another reminder that our government is always a step behind, scrambling to fix problems that should never have happened in the first place.

The immediate task for Ruto is to find these lost souls and bring them home. But let's be honest, with the way things are run here, expecting swift and effective action is probably as realistic as expecting stagnant growth forecasts to suddenly skyrocket. [Kenya Central Bank Holds Key Rate, Growth Forecasts Stagnant, Just Like Everything Else](/posts/kenya-central-bank-holds-key-rate--growth-forecasts-stagnant--just-like-everything-else)

Families are desperate for answers, for confirmation, and for some form of closure. The government needs to get its act together, verify these recruits, and ensure they are either brought home safely or their families are given the truth, no matter how ugly it is.
