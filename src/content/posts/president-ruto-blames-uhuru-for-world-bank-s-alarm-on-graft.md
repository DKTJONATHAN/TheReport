Ruto Blames Uhuru for World Bank Graft Alarm, SGR Cash Still Talking

This whole World Bank report calling Kenya a corrupt den? Well, President Ruto, ever the realist, has conveniently shifted the blame. Apparently, it’s all Uhuru’s fault, particularly the Standard Gauge Railway. He was nowhere near it, he says, and those pointing fingers probably have their hands deep in the SGR’s questionable cookie jar. Typical Nairobi politics - point, counterpoint, and the ordinary Kenyan is left holding the short end of the stick.

Ruto’s administration wants this report out in the open, so Kenyans can apparently “understand” our economic woes. But then, he accuses critics of cherry picking. So, is the report a damning indictment or a nuanced document that only he and his cronies truly grasp? The man is speaking in riddles, just like every politician before him. He was even talking about unity and how electoral contests are for the voters. Remember when [Kenya's 2027 Race Reopens Old Fault Lines: Another Round of Empty Promises](/posts/kenya-s-2027-race-reopens-old-fault-lines--another-round-of-empty-promises)? This is that same old song, just with a different choir.

And what about the clashes in Narok? Oh, that’s just political differences, not meant to spill over onto the streets. Patrick and Gabriel, both UDA, mind you. As if party affiliation stops the mudslinging and the eventual chaos. The Defence Cabinet Secretary chimes in with talk of ethnic division. It’s always about unity until it’s time to consolidate power, then it’s a free-for-all.

Other leaders, predictably, are singing Ruto’s praises, citing development. Development for whom? The same people who are still asking where the SGR money went? The same people who are told to understand economic challenges while being expected to support a second term bid? It’s a well-worn script, and the audience is getting tired.

The World Bank might be raising the alarm on graft, but the real crime is the predictable cycle of blame and empty promises that keeps this country from actually moving forward.
