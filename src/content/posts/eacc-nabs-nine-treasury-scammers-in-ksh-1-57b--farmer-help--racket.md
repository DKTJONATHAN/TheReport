So, the Ethics and Anti-Corruption Commission (EACC) has finally decided to slap some cuffs on nine folks, two of them apparently senior chaps from the National Treasury. The charge? Pilfering a cool Ksh 1.57 billion from this thing called the Programme for Rural Outreach of Financial Innovations and Technologies (PROFIT). PROFIT, eh? Looks like the only profit here was for the suspects.

This PROFIT Programme was supposed to be about helping small-scale farmers get loans. Instead, it seems it was a direct pipeline for some well-connected individuals and businesses to line their pockets. The EACC is chirping about payments for goods and services that were never delivered. Forged documents, an unauthorized bank account with Ksh 175 million just sitting there to be siphoned off, and direct financial ties between program officials and these phantom suppliers. Sounds like a classic Nairobi script.

Honestly, it’s exhausting. Every other week it’s a new scandal, a new pot of public money disappearing. These "public officials" and "business owners" are just a different breed of hyena, feasting on resources meant for actual development. Remember when we talked about university funding uncertainties? [/posts/ruto-s-university-funding-overhaul--more-uncertainty-for-broke-students] Well, at least that was a more transparent drain on the economy. This PROFIT scandal is just pure, unadulterated theft disguised as 'programme implementation'.

The EACC is going to parry about recovering funds and assets. Good luck with that. Most of it has probably already been laundered through property, offshore accounts, or spent on flashy cars and multiple homes. Twenty people are slated for prosecution, including company directors and more public officials. They'll face a laundry list of charges, from unlawful acquisition of public property to money laundering. We’ll see them in court, looking all remorseful, before they get a slap on the wrist and emerge richer than they went in.

And get this, they've even got 11 more suspects they're asking to "present themselves." As if anyone who’s busy embezzling billions will just stroll into Integrity Centre. This whole circus is just a performance for the public, a fleeting moment of supposed justice before the next act of grand larceny begins. It's the Nairobi way, a perpetual cycle of greed and betrayal.

This whole PROFIT Programme ran from 2013/2014 to 2023/2024. Ten years of systematic plunder. Ten years of farmers waiting for support that never materialized. And now, nine arrests and a whole lot of noise. Let's just hope the DPP actually pushes for some serious convictions this time, though I wouldn't hold my breath.
