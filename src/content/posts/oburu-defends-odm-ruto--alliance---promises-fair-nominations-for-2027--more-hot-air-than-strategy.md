Oburu Defends ODM-Ruto "Alliance" While Promising Fair Nominations for 2027 - Same Old Song and Dance

Oburu Oginga, deep in his 80s and on doctor's orders for bed rest - convenient, isn't it? - has decided to bless us with his wisdom from afar. Apparently, ODM's cosy arrangement with President Ruto isn't a betrayal, but a "deliberate political strategy." Translation: they're playing the long game, hoping to sneak back into power by licking the boots of the current regime. And the best part? He's promising "free and fair nominations" for 2027, especially for that Nairobi Senate seat. We’ll see about that.

This whole "broad-based government" drama is just ODM trying to spin their way out of looking like they’ve sold out. Oburu claims it’s about influencing decisions from *within*. Right. More likely, it’s about getting a piece of the pie and ensuring they’re not left out in the cold when the next government is formed. It’s all about positioning, he says, to form the *next* government. Because clearly, being in the opposition is just too much work and not nearly as profitable. It reminds me of that time [The Desperate Rebranding of Overpriced Obscurity](/posts/the-desperate-rebranding-of-overpriced-obscurity) - same old tactics, just a different name.

He’s touting the cooperation agreement signed with Ruto back in March 2025 as proof of their strategic brilliance. Governance, inclusion, devolution - all sounds lovely on paper. But let’s be real, when have these political deals ever truly benefited the common mwananchi? It’s always about the big players and their power plays. Oburu’s insisting it’s a strategic choice, not a surrender. But for anyone watching Kenyan politics, it smells an awful lot like a strategic climb-down.

And then there's the promise of fair nominations. Oburu specifically mentioned the Nairobi Senate seat, which has drawn attention with Winnie Odinga throwing her hat in the ring and Jalang'o also being mentioned. For aspirants, this is the real test. Will the nominations be as clean as Oburu’s bedside manner? Or will it be the usual Nairobi-style backroom deals and favouritism? We’ve seen [Another Day, Another Politician Gone](/posts/police-track-down-mt-elgon-politician-wasama-s-suspected-killers---another-day--another-politician-gone) - perhaps a fair nomination process is too much to ask.

Oburu also waxed lyrical about Raila's legacy, his contributions to democracy, and how ODM structures are still operational. He even mentioned mausoleums and stadiums. All well and good, but legacy doesn't pay the bills or fix the potholes. The "Enigma lives on," he declares. For now, the enigma seems to be how ODM plans to remain relevant by cosy-ing up to the current power.
