So Martha Karua, the ever-so-righteous former Justice Minister, is now telling President Ruto to pack his bags if he can't hack the job. Apparently, the "Kenyans don't want you" routine is getting tiresome, and she thinks he should be the one leaving the country, not the opposition. She claims Ruto's administration is so desperate they're bringing in "Nairobi goons" with their rungus to disrupt rallies, and get this - they're working *with* the police. She's painting a picture of a government run by thugs, with the police just standing by. Honestly, sounds about right for this country.

She's comparing these alleged goons to militias from Sudan. Big words, Martha, but then again, what else is new? It's all about scare tactics and making it sound like we're on the brink of civil war. She's urging the police, our "brothers and sisters," not to be used as tools to oppress us. Good luck with that. They've got their jobs, and we've got our… well, we've got our complaints. It’s a familiar tune, much like the endless discussions about [Gachagua's Macron Swipe: Ksh150B Kenya-France Deals During Africa Forward Summit - Another Sideshow?]( /posts/gachagua-s-macron-swipe--ksh150b-kenya-france-deals-during-africa-forward-summit---another-sideshow), where big talk often overshadows actual solutions.

Then there's the whole deportation drama from Uganda. Karua believes Ruto knew all about her being kicked out. "There was no way somebody of my class could be brought back from Uganda without our President knowing," she declared. It’s a convenient narrative, isn't it? Blame the big man for everything. She's also accusing East African governments of ganging up to suppress citizen rights, bringing up Tanzania as another example. Sounds like a regional conspiracy theory for the ages.

And as if that wasn't enough, she's digging up Ruto's past, reminding everyone about his ICC days and hinting that "witness developments" could reopen that can of worms. It's the classic political playbook: smear the opponent, remind people of their past mistakes, and paint yourself as the pristine defender of human rights. She vows not to be intimidated, locally or internationally. We'll see how long that brave front lasts when the "goons" are really out in force.
