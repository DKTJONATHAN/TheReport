COURT SAYS KENYA ELECTION DUE THIS YEAR NOT 2027, BUT DON'T HOLD YOUR BREATH

The High Court has dropped a bombshell: Kenya’s next presidential election is supposed to be in August 2026, not 2027. But before you get too excited about elections, the same court decided it’s too chaotic to actually hold one then. So, we’re stuck in limbo, with the constitution technically violated but the reality on the ground unchanged. Classic Kenya.

Judge Mugure Thande, in her infinite wisdom, declared that the Constitution clearly states the election should be on the second Tuesday of August in the fifth year after the last one. That means August 2026. She even threw in a warning that holding it any other time is unconstitutional and void. But then, she suspended that declaration until after the *next* General Election, which, conveniently, is still scheduled for 2027. So, the court says one thing, then immediately backs down, afraid of upsetting the apple cart. Sounds familiar, doesn't it? It’s like complaining about the Nairobi traffic but then refusing to ban matatus.

The petitioners, bless their hopeful hearts, wanted a clear date and an order for the IEBC to hold the poll in 2026. But the judge said, "Hold on, it's October 2025 now, and everyone's already gearing up for 2027. You want us to flip everything upside down? Impossible!" She practically admitted that enforcing the law would lead to "unmitigable national instability." Translation: The elites are already planning their 2027 campaigns, and any disruption would mess up their carefully laid plans. This whole judicial exercise feels like a performance, a way to tick the boxes of "justice" without actually changing anything for the common man, whose pockets are always empty. Just like when [Otile Brown Calls Out Leaders' Silence On Missing Children: What's New?](/posts/otile-brown-calls-out-leaders--silence-on-missing-children--what-s-new?), our leaders are good at making noise but useless when it comes to real action.

The judge also clarified that the President’s term isn't a guaranteed five years, citing past elections where terms were cut short. She even pointed out that the August 9, 2022 election happened before Uhuru Kenyatta completed his full five years. So, the constitutional timetable for elections takes precedence. Yet, despite this, the court won't force the IEBC to act. It’s a classic case of "rules for thee, but not for me." The court basically said, "We see the defect, but fixing it is too much trouble."

So, here we are. The court has acknowledged the election should have been in 2026, but it’s not going to happen. The IEBC has been given a chance to "correct" a constitutional defect that they were never going to be forced to fix anyway. The pronouncement that any election outside August 2026 would be null and void is now subject to a suspension that basically renders it meaningless until the 2027 election, which everyone was already preparing for. It’s a legal charade designed to make it look like the courts are working, while in reality, the political calendar remains firmly on track for 2027.
