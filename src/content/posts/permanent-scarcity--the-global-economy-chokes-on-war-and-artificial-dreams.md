While oil prices fluctuate based on the latest false hope of peace agreements, the underlying infrastructure is being systematically dismantled. The destruction of refining facilities across Russia and the Middle East has sent crack spreads to all-time highs. This means that even if crude oil prices stabilize, the cost of the actual fuel that runs the world remains prohibitively expensive. It is a structural shift toward permanent scarcity, one that has already cost developing nations like Kenya billions of dollars in a matter of months, proving that the economic discomfort of the global north is an agonizing death rattle for the global south.

Then there is the matter of the Great AI Mirage. Equity markets have been kept on life support by a handful of semiconductor stocks, yet the cracks are finally showing. The recent correction in technology stocks reveals a fragile ecosystem built on the hope of infinite returns from artificial intelligence. With Chinese models now matching US performance at a fraction of the cost, the profit margins of the West's latest "savior" industry are under siege. The leveraged investment products tied to these tech hyperscalers are a ticking time bomb, threatening to turn a market correction into a full-scale systemic collapse.

Inflation anchoring is the new secular religion, but the parishioners are losing faith. Even as central banks prepare for more rate hikes in late 2026 and early 2027, the cost of living continues its relentless climb. Food prices are rising, driven by El Nino and the scorched earth of European heatwaves. The ECB can move interest rates by twenty-five basis points all they want, but they cannot print more grain or refine more diesel. We are entering an era where monetary policy is increasingly irrelevant against the raw reality of resource depletion and perpetual warfare.
