2026 Commonwealth Games: Kenya Women's Basketball's 'Date with Destiny' is Probably Just Another Snooze Fest











So, the ladies are off to Glasgow for the 2026 Commonwealth Games, with their "date with destiny" set for Sunday. Apparently, they need to beat Jamaica and the Cayman Islands to snag a quarter-final spot. This is after they managed a lukewarm 16-14 win against Fiji, which is apparently a "lifeline". A lifeline. Right. Never mind the identical 21-5 beatings they took from Australia and Uganda. Typical.

Meanwhile, the men's team, bless their optimistic hearts, kicked off their campaign by losing to Singapore. They were apparently "on course for victory" at one point, leading by three. Then, what do you know, they choked. Just like in politics, where promises are made and then conveniently forgotten, like that [March 7 Aftermath: Who Really Won in the Ruto-Raila Broad-Based Govt Pact? Don't Hold Your Breath.](/posts/march-7-aftermath--who-really-won-in-the-ruto-raila-broad-based-govt-pact--don-t-hold-your-breath) deal. Captain Dennis Aduol Koja apparently missed two free throws when it mattered. Classic.

Rodney Sande, the guy who’s supposed to be “optimistic,” is spouting the usual nonsense about "adjusting" and "seeing our mistakes." We’ve seen your mistakes, Rodney. They’re called missed shots and a general lack of competitive edge against teams that actually know how to play. The 3x3 format, a frantic dash to 21 points, leaves no room for these amateurish lapses.

The men’s team still has to face Malaysia and the hosts, England. Sande believes they can "produce an upset." Oh, yes, the power of national pride against a home crowd and a presumably better-prepared team. Let’s just hope they don’t embarrass themselves too badly. Remember 2022? Third in the pool, then a quick exit. History, as they say, repeats itself, especially when you don't learn.

The women's team's "thrilling" victory against Fiji sounds more like a desperate scramble than genuine dominance. Fiji isn't exactly a global basketball powerhouse. This whole "date with destiny" sounds more like a looming disappointment. We’ll see them back here, nursing their bruised egos and blaming the referee or the jet lag. Just another sporting spectacle to distract us from the real issues, isn't it?
