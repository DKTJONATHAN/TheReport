Vera Sidika Flies to Armenia: Because Kenya Isn't Enough?

The "Kenya to the World" narrative gets a fresh coat of gloss, as Vera Sidika, the queen of all things shiny and self-promotional, bags a Platinum Guest invite to some summit in Armenia. Apparently, the land of Mount Ararat and ancient monasteries needs a dose of Kenyan lifestyle, beauty, and whatever else she peddles online. Five hundred creators from over a hundred countries, all there to talk about visibility and storytelling. I'm sure the stories will be riveting, especially if they involve luxury handbags and strategically angled selfies.

This isn't just about a holiday, mind you. For Sidika, it's about extending her brand beyond the usual suspects, proving her audience has value even when they're not buying products advertised on Kenyan Instagram. The organizers are gushing about her "lifestyle content and influence." It’s a smart move, this international exposure. It’s always easier to get noticed when you’re a big fish in a pond that doesn’t know your usual circles. Forget local endorsements; the real money is probably in convincing some Armenian tourism board that their ancient ruins need a touch of modern glamour.

The whole digital creator industry is getting "increasingly commercial," they say. As if it wasn't already. This whole event sounds like a high-end networking mixer for people who excel at looking busy doing nothing important. Access to brands, agencies, and potential partners from different markets. It’s the global version of that friend who’s always inviting you to "exclusive" events where you end up paying for overpriced drinks. Still, if it means more cash for Sidika, who am I to complain? We all have our hustles, even if hers involves jetting off to Armenia. Maybe it’s like when the peacemaker clocks out; everyone just does their own thing, and sometimes that involves international travel.

Ultimately, it’s another reminder that for some, the Kenyan market is just a stepping stone. While others are out here wrestling with political dramas or trying to make sense of things like Riggy G's new gospel, some are busy expanding their empires one continent at a time. Armenia. Who would have thought? At least she's not claiming to solve the world's problems. Just selling herself, one international summit at a time. Good for her, I guess. Just don't expect me to buy any of the merchandise.
