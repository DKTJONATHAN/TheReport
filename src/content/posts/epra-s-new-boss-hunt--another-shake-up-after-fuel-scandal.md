EPRA'S NEW BOSS HUNT: ANOTHER SHAKE UP AFTER FUEL SCANDAL

The Energy and Petroleum Regulatory Authority (EPRA) is back to its old tricks, advertising for a new Director General months after someone else was shoved into the acting role. Apparently, the last guy either jumped or was pushed after that little ‘fuel quality’ drama that cost us billions. Now they’re looking for a ‘suitably qualified Kenyan’ to steer the ship, as if Kenya’s got a shortage of people eager to get their hands on the till.

This whole song and dance is just the predictable fallout from that Ksh4 billion fuel scandal. Remember that? When they swore the imported fuel was just fine, but it turned out to be rubbish? Classic. Now, Daniel Kiptoo is out, along with some other big names, and they’ve got Joseph Oketch doing the interim gig. Let's hope his ‘acting’ skills are better than the fuel quality they approved.

The job requirements are enough to make your eyes water: a degree in something vaguely scientific or economic, seven years of ‘senior management’ experience (read: glad-handing and looking busy), and a two-year stint in the oil and gas world. They want someone to provide ‘strategic leadership’ and ‘strengthen governance’. Oh, the irony. It’s like asking a fox to guard a henhouse, especially after the last heist. This feels eerily similar to the ghost worker scandal in Garissa, where official positions seemed to be more about lining pockets than doing actual work.

So, while EPRA pretends to be cleaning house, they’re really just going through the motions. They’ll pick someone, they’ll sign a three-year contract, and then what? Another scandal down the line, another resignation, and another hunt for a new ‘boss’. This is Nairobi, after all. The only thing guaranteed is that the prices at the pump will keep climbing, and our wallets will keep shrinking. It’s the circle of Kenyan life.
