DCI Nabs 15 Thugs on Highway With Crude Weapons - Just Another Sunday

WARNING: Another bunch of potential troublemakers hauled in, but don't expect any miracles. The cycle of hired muscle and political drama continues.

A grey Toyota Noah and two white vans, packed with 15 goons and their shiny new pangas and knives, were intercepted by the DCI on the Kisumu-Kericho highway. Apparently, someone tipped them off, and the Operations Support Unit, Crime Research and Intelligence Bureau, and Kisumu Central DCI all showed up to play grab-a-thug. It’s a good haul, I guess, but this is just the tip of the iceberg.

The usual suspects were found: mobile phones (probably for coordinating the next mayhem), enough machetes and pangas to start a small farm, ropes (for tying up who knows what), and a metal rod for good measure. The DCI is calling it a "meticulously coordinated operation," which is code for "they got lucky." They recovered the goods and carted off the 15, who are now waiting to be processed.

Naturally, one "leader" managed to slip through their fingers. Because of course he did. The DCI is now appealing to the public for help, as if anyone wants to get involved in this mess. Meanwhile, the whispers are that these chaps were headed to Homa Bay for some "Linda Mwananchi rally" disruption. Whether that's true or just more political theatre, well, who knows? As we’ve seen with [HR Folders And Dead-End Dockets: Why Your Boss Is Getting Away With It](/posts/hr-folders-and-dead-end-dockets--why-your-boss-is-getting-away-with-it), justice can be a slippery thing.

The police are quite keen on pointing out that these hired goons are usually ferried in from neighbouring counties. It’s a convenient narrative, isn't it? Blame the outsiders for the mess the local politicians stir up. It’s a tired story, and these arrests just add another footnote to the ongoing saga of political violence and the hired hands who carry it out.

The 15 suspects are now in custody, their crude weapons confiscated. The search for the one who escaped is on. But honestly, it’s a drop in the ocean. Nairobi sees this kind of thing every other day. These "gang members" are just pawns in a much bigger, and much uglier, game.
