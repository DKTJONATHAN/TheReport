So, they're trotting out another list, are they? "Kenya's Richest Counties By Contribution to GDP [2026]". Because that's exactly what we all need to know - which parts of the country are doing well so the rest of us can feel even worse about our own stagnant bank accounts. It’s a predictable song and dance, really. The same old players in the same old positions, while the rest of us are left scrambling for crumbs.

Money254, bless their diligent hearts, are telling us this information is “publicly available” and for “educational purposes.” Sure. Just like that time they published that article about Ruto's 'Revenue Optimisation' - another scheme that probably benefited a select few while the masses choked on new taxes. This GDP list is just another way to highlight the massive chasm between the haves and the have-nots, all wrapped up in a neat little package.

They even include the obligatory disclaimer, a classic move. "Money254 strives to keep the information... up to date and does not warranty that all the information will be as the financial institutions updated or changed information at all times." Translation: Don't get too attached to these numbers, they'll probably be irrelevant by lunchtime. And heaven forbid you make a decision based on this - it's your funeral. They're not liable, remember? Just like those financial institutions they "represent" can change their minds faster than a chameleon on a rainbow.

Let's be real. This isn't about empowering the common mwananchi. It's about showcasing the established order, the counties that already have the infrastructure, the political clout, and the pre-existing wealth. It's a visual confirmation that the game is rigged, and these statistics are just the scoreboard for the victors. Don't expect any surprises here; it's the same old story of Nairobi and a handful of others hogging the spotlight, while the rest of us are just… existing.

And in case you're wondering, they're not a financial institution, insurance provider, or financial adviser. So, basically, they're just a middleman providing information that might be outdated by the time you read it, and they can't be blamed when your hopes of financial betterment crumble like a stale mandazi. It's a classic Nairobi hustle, this providing of "information" with zero accountability.

So, while you pore over these "richest counties," remember who’s really benefiting. It’s not you, unless you’re already living in one of those golden enclaves. For the rest of us, it’s just another reminder that the path to prosperity in Kenya is a lot more exclusive than these glossy lists would have you believe.
