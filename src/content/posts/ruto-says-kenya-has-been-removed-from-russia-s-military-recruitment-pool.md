Ruto Says Kenya Off Russia's Military Recruitment List - But Who's Really Benefiting?
Ruto claims a diplomatic win, saying Kenyans are no longer being signed up by Russia for their military. But let's not get ahead of ourselves, this is Nairobi, and we know how these deals *really* work.
At least the President is trying to get our boys back home, though the thought of them signing up for a war in the first place is just...peak Nairobi.
Politics, Kenya, Foreign Relations
Ruto, Russia, Military
Diplomacy

So, President William Ruto is back from his Moscow jaunt, patting himself on the back because Vladmir Putin apparently agreed to stop enlisting Kenyans into his war machine. "Kenya is a non-source country now," he chirps, as if this is some kind of badge of honor. We’ve gone from being a recruitment ground to being… what exactly? An inconvenience? A country that finally realized sending its own citizens to die for someone else's fight is bad business?

The government is now talking about appointing a defence attaché to "coordinate the return" of these lost souls. Coordinate the return. As if they’re lost luggage. We’re talking about Kenyans who were probably promised a fat paycheck and a glorious future, only to find themselves dodging bullets in Ukraine. This isn't a triumphant homecoming; it's damage control for a decision that should never have been made. We're still cleaning up the mess, just like we're still grappling with the debt from [Kenya SGR to Kisumu: More Tracks, More Debt, Same Old Story](/posts/kenya-sgr-to-kisumu--more-tracks--more-debt--same-old-story).

And the numbers? Mudavadi's figures of 354 Kenyans "connected to the conflict" - that includes the dead, the missing, the hospitalized, and those who miraculously made it back. It's a grim tally, a testament to our government's belated attempt to pull our people out of a fire they were encouraged, or at least allowed, to walk into. The "stop list" they're so proud of securing? It sounds less like a protection measure and more like a bureaucratic afterthought.

But hey, it's not all bad news for our esteemed leaders. Ruto also managed to squeeze in talks about more scholarships for Kenyan students, particularly in fancy fields like AI and cybersecurity. Because what we *really* need is more Kenyans trained in high-tech warfare, I suppose. And Putin is even going to support the UN office in Nairobi. Wonderful. All this while our people were being used as cannon fodder. It's a classic Nairobi manoeuvre: fix one problem by creating a distraction, or perhaps by making it look like you fixed it, all while securing more "cooperation."

Let's not forget the five instruments signed to strengthen bilateral relations. Intergovernmental commissions, labour inspection, employment promotion - it all sounds very official and very important. Meanwhile, the real agreement seems to be that Kenya can now send more of its citizens to work in Russia, provided it’s not for military purposes. Because clearly, the labour market there is just booming for Kenyans, never mind the ongoing geopolitical turmoil. It's all about the optics, isn't it? Pretend to be concerned, make a deal, and carry on with business as usual. Just another day in paradise.
