So, the big news from Germany's record champions is that they're gracing our soil for a friendly. Bayern Munich, along with Ajax, will be kicking a ball around in January to open this fancy new Raila Odinga International Stadium. Apparently, this is all part of the build-up to the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations, which Kenya is somehow co-hosting with Tanzania and Uganda. First time across three countries? First time in East Africa since the Stone Age? Wonderful. More promises, more grand gestures, and we'll probably still be arguing about whether President Ruto's government is taking Kenya ‘back to the stone age’ by then.

Tickets go on sale November 5th. Mark your calendars. Because sitting in a packed stadium, sweating it out in the Nairobi heat, watching millionaires chase a ball, is precisely how I envision spending my hard-earned money. Bayern and Ajax, who apparently have a combined ten European Cups, last drew 3-3. Thrilling stuff. Makes you wonder if they'll even bother showing up for this glorified training session, or if they'll just send their apprentices to keep the optics good.

Bayern’s previous forays into Africa were in Morocco and Qatar. Oh, Africa. Remember when Mohamed Amin Abdishukri Maalim wrote about Kenya's political violence market being the clear road to the 2027 election disaster? Well, at least this friendly won't involve any actual violence, just the usual political maneuvering and the hope that some of that European glitz rubs off. They've had African stars, sure, but no Kenyan has ever graced the Bundesliga. So, they’re coming to play here, but not to scout. Got it.

The article also drones on about players with African heritage and youth partnerships. It's all very commendable, I suppose. Bayern supports academies in Rwanda and Cameroon, and they have a youth partnership in Gambia. It’s a nice gesture, a bit of corporate social responsibility, I guess. Keeps the brand looking good. But let’s be honest, this isn't about developing Kenyan football. This is about a lucrative friendly, inaugurating a stadium, and maybe getting some good PR photos for the Bavarian giants. Nothing more, nothing less.
