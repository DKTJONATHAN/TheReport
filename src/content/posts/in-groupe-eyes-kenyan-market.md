**IN Groupe Eyes Kenyan Market: Digital ID Dreams, Real-World Scams**

So, Kenya is apparently talking to some French company, IN Groupe, about a fancy new digital identity platform. Cabinet Secretary William Kabogo Gitau is all smiles, talking about secure access to services, no more endless paperwork, and making life easier for us regular folks. Sounds lovely, doesn't it? Like a fairy tale where everyone lives happily ever after with their perfectly verified digital selves. But we've heard this song and dance before, haven't we?

This whole "trusted and interoperable digital identity ecosystem" sounds like another way for someone to get rich while we get more data tracked and less privacy. They promise to eliminate duplication and maximize investments, but what they really mean is centralize control and probably open the backdoor for more data leaks. Remember how those promises about efficiency and transparency usually pan out? It’s like trying to fix El Niño's water chaos with a leaky bucket.

They're bringing in French expertise to build this digital utopia. "Building public trust and ensuring value for money" are the buzzwords, of course. But when have we ever seen value for money in these massive ICT procurements? It's always about the slick presentations and the promises of future benefits, while the immediate costs are hidden and the long-term benefits are… well, let's just say they tend to evaporate.

The Ministry of ICT and the Digital Economy will be the "overarching governance" body, apparently. This is just a fancy way of saying they'll be the ones signing off on the cheques and taking the heat when it all goes sideways. They've "identified immediate areas of action that will realise benefits for Kenyan youth, innovators and technology professionals alike." Translation: some lucky few will get kickbacks, and the rest of us will be left with a system that’s either clunky, insecure, or both.

This whole digital ID push feels less like progress and more like another opportunity for rent-seeking. While they're busy playing with digital identities, we're still dealing with the same old inefficiencies and corruption that plague everything else. Let's see how long this "trusted ecosystem" lasts before it becomes another digital ghost town.
