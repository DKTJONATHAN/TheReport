So, Kevin Bahati, the man who’s apparently decided 10 years together warrants a KSh1 million performance at his wedding, is now on the hunt for a singer. November 6, 2026, is the big day, or as I call it, another opportunity for the rich and famous to flaunt their cash. “The Bahatis’ Big Day,” they’re calling it. More like “The Bahatis’ Big Spend,” if you ask me.

This whole shindig, dubbed “The Invite,” is apparently an exclusive black-tie affair. They’re sending out invitations like they’re handing out business cards in this city, and guess what? Some people are actually paying for them. Phoina Tosha apparently coughed up KSh2 million for two invites. For what? To watch Bahati tie the knot? Meanwhile, KRG The Don, a “Kenyan prince” and forex trader, was the first to buy one. Makes sense, I suppose. It’s all about the networking, right?

Bahati’s story about meeting Diana during a music video shoot in 2016 sounds like something out of a cheesy movie. He remembered her because she liked one of his Instagram photos. Five weeks later, she was his girlfriend. And now, after a decade and a brood of five children - including his adopted son and his firstborn daughter from a previous relationship - they’re making it official. It’s the kind of fairy tale that makes you wonder if there’s any real struggle left in this world, or if it’s all just for show. Speaking of struggles, you can read about how some Kenyans are facing building collapse dread as the rains loom. And just when you thought the wedding wasn't enough of a spectacle, Bahati releases a new track, "Uinuliwe." Apparently, it’s a "gratitude letter to the Lord" inspired by the upcoming wedding. Because nothing screams gratitude like a KSh1 million performance fee and a guest list that reads like a who's who of Nairobi's elite. Let's just hope the musician he picks for a cool million can actually sing, otherwise, that’s a lot of money for a glorified karaoke session.
