SO, WHO WAS JOSEPH MUTAVA, THE FORMER JUDGE WHOSE CAREER WENT FROM ZERO TO HERO AND BACK TO ZERO?

FORMER HIGH COURT JUDGE JOSEPH MUTAVA HAS KICKED THE BUCKET IN MAKUENI COUNTY, AND WHILE POLITICIANS ARE BUSY SHEDDING CROCODILE TEARS ABOUT HIS "LEGACY" AND "SERVICE," LET'S TALK ABOUT THE REAL STORY BEHIND THE MAN. THIS WASN'T JUST A SUDDEN ILLNESS; THIS WAS THE END OF A CAREER THAT PROMISED A LOT BUT DELIVERED SCANDAL, ESPECIALLY HIS DEALINGS WITH THE INFAMOUS GOLDENBERG SCANDAL SUSPECT, KAMLESH PATTNI.

MUTAVA, MARRIED TO A PARLIAMENTARIAN NO LESS, DECIDED TO CHECK OUT HOURS AFTER A FUNERAL, GETTING RUSHED TO A PRIVATE HOSPITAL ONLY TO BE PRONOUNCED DEAD ON ARRIVAL. HIS WIFE, MS. MBALU, WAS, OF COURSE, "HEARTBROKEN." BUT THE REAL HEARTBREAK WAS FOR KENYANS WHO HAD TO ENDURE JUDGES LIKE HIM, WHOSE APPOINTMENT IN 2011 BY PRESIDENT KIBAKI WAS SUPPOSED TO BE A MARK OF EXCELLENCE. HE GRADUATED FIRST CLASS, WORKED AT THE CENTRAL BANK - SOUNDED GOOD ON PAPER, DIDN'T IT?

BUT THE BENCH WAS A DIFFERENT STORY. BETWEEN MARCH 2012 AND MARCH 2013 ALONE, THE JUDICIAL SERVICE COMMISSION HAD TO DEAL WITH THIRTEEN COMPLAINTS ABOUT HIS CONDUCT. THIRTEEN! AND GUESS WHAT? THREE WERE SERIOUS ENOUGH FOR A FULL INVESTIGATION. THE BIGGEST WHIFF CAME FROM THE KAMLESH PATTNI CASE, THE GOLDENBERG SCANDAL GUY. MUTAVA, IT SEEMS, DECIDED TO TAKE THE CASE HIMSELF, WITHOUT ANYONE ELSE KNOWING, AND THEN WROTE THE JUDGMENT WHILE THE JSC WAS ALREADY EYEING HIM SUSPICIOUSLY. AND TO TOP IT OFF, HE RULED IN PATTNI'S FAVOR, STOPPING THE CRIMINAL PROCEEDINGS. YES, THE SAME PATTNI WHO WAS TRYING TO CUT DEALS AFTER SURRENDERING A HOTEL. TALK ABOUT THE BLIND EYE.

THE JUDICIAL SYSTEM, EVER SO SLOW, GOT CAUGHT UP IN ITS OWN BUREAUCRACY. MUTAVA CHALLENGED THE INVESTIGATION AND WON IN THE HIGH COURT, BUT THE JSC APPEALED, AND THE COURT OF APPEAL BACKED THE INVESTIGATION. FINALLY, A TRIBUNAL SAID HE SHOULD BE REMOVED, BUT MUTAVA WENT TO THE SUPREME COURT. THE SUPREME COURT SAID NO, HE WAS GUILTY OF SERIOUS MISCONDUCT AND OUT HE WENT IN 2019. REMEMBER HOW CORRUPTION CREEPS INTO SYSTEMS LIKE THESE? IT REMINDS ME OF THE ROT WE SEE IN SO MANY PLACES, LIKE [The Anatomy of a Political Assassination: How Baltasar Engonga's Fall Reveals Africa's Corruption Cancer](/posts/sex-power-videos-and-baltasar-ebang-engonga).

AFTER HIS DISMISSAL, MUTAVA WENT QUIET, RUNNING A LAW FIRM. BUT HIS SHADOW LOOMED, AND IN 2026, HIS NAME POPPED UP AGAIN IN A SH15 MILLION BRIBERY SCANDAL INVOLVING FORMER CS RAPHAEL TUJU. ALLEGATIONS OF INTERMEDIARIES TRYING TO INFLUENCE CASES. THESE THINGS WERE STILL UNDER INVESTIGATION WHEN HE DIED. SO MUCH FOR "HONOUR, INTEGRITY AND QUIET DISTINCTION" THAT KALONZO MUSYOKA IS BLOODY PRATTING ON ABOUT. AND THE GOVERNOR TALKING ABOUT HIS "LEGACY" IN COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT? PLEASE. THIS IS KENYA; WE SEE THE REALITY BEHIND THE SMOOTH TALK.

FORMER JUDGE MUTAVA'S DEATH CLOSES A CHAPTER, BUT THE SCARS ON OUR JUDICIARY REMAIN. HIS CAREER WAS A PRIME EXAMPLE OF HOW FAST SOME CAN RISE, ONLY TO BE BROUGHT DOWN BY THEIR OWN GREED AND CORRUPTION.
