So, Rigathi Gachagua is back in church, this time in Karatina, Nyeri. As if the Almighty Himself cares about the 2027 General Election. His Democratic Party for Citizens (DCP) is apparently "expanding grassroots activities," which in plain Swahili means shaking hands, kissing babies, and promising heaven on earth for votes. Reverend Dorcas Rigathi tagging along? Probably to bless the envelopes.

This little pilgrimage comes right after some "public engagements" in Embu. It’s all part of the grand strategy, you see. Mount Kenya is the battleground, and Gachagua is planting his flag, or rather, his prayer mats. He’s scheduled for a "meet-the-people tour" in Kirinyaga next, hosted by Senator James Murango and other DCP loyalists. Expect the usual song and dance at Kiangagana, Kiangombe, and Rurui shopping centres. They’re even calling on residents to "turn up." As if people have nothing better to do than attend a political rally disguised as a community visit.

Adding a bit of spice to this political stew is Purity Ngirici jumping ship from UDA to DCP. Her social media announcement on August 12th is just another testament to how disposable these political allegiances are. It's all about who's offering the best seat at the table, or perhaps, who's got the deeper pockets. This move certainly beefs up Gachagua's network, proving that in Kenyan politics, loyalty is as stable as a government imprest. It’s all a game of musical chairs, and the voters are the ones left standing when the music stops.

The whole charade is quite predictable. Sunday church services have become the new town hall meetings. While Gachagua was busy in Nyeri, President Ruto was doing his own divine duty in Taita Taveta. It’s a calculated move, using faith as a shield for ambition. They’re all trying to consolidate support, defining their "political paths" while conveniently forgetting the actual issues facing ordinary Kenyans. It's a well-worn playbook, and frankly, it's exhausting. This constant jockeying for position, this relentless pursuit of power, it reminds me of how the elite often get fleeced in the shadows, while pretending to be on the up and up. With 2027 breathing down our necks, expect more of this religious theatre and backroom deals. Mount Kenya is the prize, and everyone's scrambling for a piece of the pie. They’ll promise you the world, paint saints out of themselves, and then disappear once the votes are cast. Just remember, when these leaders attend church, they’re not just seeking salvation; they’re strategizing for their next ascent to power, and we, the voters, are merely pawns in their perpetual game.
