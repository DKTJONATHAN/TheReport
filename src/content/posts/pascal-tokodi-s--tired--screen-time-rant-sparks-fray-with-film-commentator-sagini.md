Pascal Tokodi Shoots Back At Sagini Over "Tired" Screen Time Complaints

Some film commentator, Sagini Megane, has decided to make a name for himself by whinging about seeing the same faces on Kenyan screens. Apparently, he’s had it up to here with Pascal Tokodi, Sarah Hassan, Brenda Wairimu, Catherine Kamau, and Blessing Lung’aho. My advice? If you’re that easily bored, maybe stick to watching paint dry. This whole "support local" song and dance is great, but if the product is repetitive, what do you expect? Don't tell me to choke down lukewarm ugali just because it's from Mama Pendo's kitchen.

Tokodi, bless his dramatic heart, has responded by calling Sagini a "loser" and questioning his "credentials." Oh, the hubris! As if being a commentator means you have to have personally sweated over a hot film set. Sagini’s point is valid: make us *want* to see you, don’t just expect us to tune in because you’re Kenyan. It's the same tired argument we hear from politicians who demand loyalty without delivering results. Remember the whole song and dance about the [March 7 Aftermath: Who Really Won in the Ruto-Raila Broad-Based Govt Pact? Don't Hold Your Breath](/posts/march-7-aftermath--who-really-won-in-the-ruto-raila-broad-based-govt-pact--don-t-hold-your-breath)? People are tired of being told to believe when the reality is far less inspiring.

Tokodi's follow-up plea to "support local creatives" while the industry is "building" is just a smokescreen for mediocrity. Yes, creating films is hard. So is running a business, or raising a family, or trying to navigate this city’s chaotic matatu system. That doesn't excuse churning out the same old predictable plots with the same old predictable actors. It’s not about perfection from day one; it’s about evolution. And right now, the evolution seems to be stuck on repeat.

He also suggests Sagini is building his platform by "tearing others down." Newsflash, Pascal: criticism is part of the entertainment industry. It’s how things get better. If you can’t handle a little shade from someone who’s *not* getting paid to be on your show, maybe you’re in the wrong business. The idea that we should just blindly support everything Kenyan, regardless of quality, is frankly insulting. It's the same logic that allows some of these religious figures to get away with murder, acting like their pronouncements are infallible because they wear a collar. [Religion: The Ultimate Get-Out-of-Jail-Free Card for Failed Politicians](/posts/religion--the-ultimate-get-out-of-jail-free-card-for-failed-politicians).

Tokodi’s closing statement, "Let's build. Then improve. In that order," is a cop-out. You can’t just build something shoddy and expect improvement to magically happen later. You build with a vision for improvement from the start. Sagini is right to call out the stagnation. Maybe if more actors like Tokodi were less defensive and more introspective, we’d see actual progress, not just more talk about how hard it is to make movies.
