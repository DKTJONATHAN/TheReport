So, these two British boys, Mohamud Siyad Abdihakim and Monteiro Tariq Mangal, thought they were slick, running from a murder charge in London and jetting into Nairobi in 2020. Wanted for the brutal stabbing of 16-year-old Alex Smith, members of the "Active" gang, they figured Kenya was their golden ticket. They even had the nerve to mock their victim on video. Pathetic, really.

Here in Nairobi, Monteiro, the younger one at 21, decided to reinvent himself as "Abdulahi Abshir Mohamed" with a fake Somali ID. He fancied himself a rapper, going by "Swavy" - talk about bitter irony. His songs, racking up millions of views, were basically confessionals boasting about their UK crimes and how they got away with it. Honestly, the arrogance. They lived in a swanky Kilimani apartment, flaunted their wealth on Instagram with trips to Diani and Samburu, and generally lived the high life we mere mortals can only dream of. It’s the predictable fallout of human frailty, isn't it?

But here's the punchline: while they were busy living large and updating their Instagram stories, the British authorities were also scrolling. They were desperate to catch these two, and these social media narcissists practically handed them the keys to the kingdom. Despite their efforts to be vague, Monteiro’s fateful kitchen photo, complete with a distinctly Kenyan Mika oven, was the clue they needed. Who knew a toaster could be so incriminating?

Once the Brits realized Mika was a local brand, they called in the DCI. Suddenly, their lavish Nairobi lifestyle became their prison. Immigration officers, who "participated in the tracking," practically admitted they were just following the breadcrumbs of their Instagram posts and holiday snaps. The Metropolitan Police had an international arrest warrant out, and these two weren't going to outsmart a motivated DCI and a bit of electronic appliance recognition.

They were finally nabbed at Al Dar Apartments on January 26, 2022. Within three days, they were extradited back to the UK to face the music. Monteiro was caught with that fake Somali ID and a dagger, just in case the fancy rap career didn't pan out. The DCI confirmed it all.

And the final act? On May 12, 2023, the UK court handed them life sentences. Monteiro got a minimum of 24 years, Mohamud 23. Alex Smith's mother, Beatriz, called him her "precious baby boy." The judge, summing it up, called it another "needless loss of a teenage life." So much for living large and getting away with it. This is what happens when you think you’re untouchable.
