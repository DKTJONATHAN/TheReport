Kindiki's Plea to Mt Kenya: Don't Let 2027 Politics Tear You Apart, Or Else

Deputy President Kithure Kindiki is out here, pleading with the Mt Kenya region to just hold hands and sing kumbaya, as if their political differences are some minor squabbles over who gets the last chapati. He's saying elections are temporary, but unity is forever. Cute. As if we haven't seen communities torn to shreds over "temporary" political contests before. He wants them to focus on development, which is rich coming from someone who's knee deep in the political horse trading.

Kindiki's speech at Gaichanjiru Catholic Church sounds like a broken record. Politics as a competition, not a blood feud. No insults, no violence, no property destruction. Because apparently, those who lose will magically get another chance at leadership without scarring the nation. He reminds them how Mt Kenya has always been united, working together in business and culture. Oh, the sweet nostalgia. Never mind the current "growing political wrangles" over whether they should stick with Ruto or go their own way, or the whispers about consolidating support around an "alternative political formation." It’s a classic Nairobi drama, really.

Nominated MP Sabina Chege is also on stage, assuring everyone that "the mountain will not be divided." She’s telling people not to be fooled by whispers, that they are all one. She even throws in a jab about voters making their own decisions and assessing leaders by their records, not party affiliations. Meanwhile, Energy Principal Secretary Alex Wachira is busy pushing Kindiki as the next big thing for the presidency in 2032. So much for unity being above political competition, eh? It seems the only thing that will endure after the elections is the scramble for power, just like the shady dealings that can lead to KENYA ENERGY BIG SHOTS 'RESIGN' OVER SHADY FUEL DEAL.

It’s all a predictable charade. Leaders preach unity while their allies are busy carving up the political landscape, eyeing future spoils. They talk about development, but the real agenda is always about who controls the levers of power. The Mt Kenya region is a battleground, and everyone knows it. The talk of unity is just a thin veil over the realpolitik of who gets what and when. They want you to vote as a bloc, then they want you to vote as individuals. Make up your minds, people.

Kindiki vows to defend the nation's unity with all his might, resources, and intellect. That sounds impressive, doesn't it? But let's be real, "might," "resources," and "intellect" are precisely what get deployed when powerful individuals are vying for control. The "peace of the community and the peace of the nation" are nice platitudes, but they're often the first casualties when political ambitions run wild.

So, while Kindiki and Chege are busy promising a unified mountain, the real story is the jockeying for position ahead of 2027 and beyond. The PS's endorsement of Kindiki for 2032 is the most honest thing said so far, revealing the underlying succession game. The rest is just noise to keep the masses distracted while the real players make their moves.
