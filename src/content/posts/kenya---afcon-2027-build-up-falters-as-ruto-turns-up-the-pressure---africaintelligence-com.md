KENYA'S AFCON 2027 DREAM FALTERS AS RUTO TURNS UP THE HEAT

Ruto's meddling in AFCON preparations and shaky grip on central Kenya politics spell trouble.

President Ruto's relentless pursuit of political advantage is, unsurprisingly, causing more chaos than progress.

POLITICS

AFCON, Ruto, Kenya

FOOTBALL

The President's entourage is desperately trying to charm the Presbyterian Church in central Kenya, a region where his support is dwindling faster than a politician's promise. Meanwhile, he's busy buttering up the west, using religious circles as his personal lobbying firm. It’s all about power, as usual.

And the Confederation of African Football? They're busy playing musical chairs with their general secretary. The interim guy, Samson Adamu, has been in the hot seat since March, but this whole recruitment process, launched under shady circumstances and with a July 10 deadline, has the folks in Cairo scratching their heads. Typical African bureaucracy, isn't it?

Back home, the head of state has given the semi-public airline a measly six months to pull a rabbit out of a hat and fix its strategy. He's betting on the directors' connections in both the private and political world to resurrect a company that went back into the red last year. Good luck with that. This whole situation reminds me of how some people in power seem to think throwing money at a problem, like that "$5 BILLION SAND TRAP" project, magically solves anything.

Meanwhile, Africa Intelligence has managed to peek at an inspection report from the continent's football governing body. While they're not exactly screaming "fire," the document does point out quite a few delays and slip-ups that need sorting before August. So, while Ruto is busy playing politics and trying to salvage a failing airline, the AFCON 2027 build-up is looking more like a slow-motion disaster. Just another day in paradise.
