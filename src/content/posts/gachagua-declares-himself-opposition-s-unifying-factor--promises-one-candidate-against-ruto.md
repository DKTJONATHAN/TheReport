Gachagua Thinks He's the Opposition's Savior, Promises One Candidate Against Ruto









So, Rigathi Gachagua, the man who used to be second-in-command and now leads some "Democracy for the Citizens Party" (sounds original), is suddenly the opposition's big unifier. He's offering to "shelve his presidential ambitions" - a sacrifice that probably costs him nothing, given the slim chances. Apparently, the only way to beat President Ruto in 2027 is to field one candidate, which is news to everyone who's been watching this circus for years. He’s calling himself the "opposition unifying factor," as if anyone asked him to step up.

Gachagua’s out here dismissing rumours of cracks in the opposition as "wishful thinking" from Ruto's camp. He claims the recent Ol Kalou by-election defeat has them "panicked." The "noise" he hears, he says, is just "friendly fire." All the big names, including Senator Edwin Sifuna’s "Linda Mwananchi," have supposedly agreed on the single candidate formula. The only disagreement is *how* to pick this mythical candidate and *when* to announce him. It’s all "internal democracy," not division. Right. Just like how the Royal family insists their family gatherings are just "robust discussions" when they’re clearly fighting over who gets the corgi. [The Royal PR Circus: Putting Lipstick on a Windsor Pig](/posts/the-royal-pr-circus--putting-lipstick-on-a-windsor-pig).

He’s also got tales of government operatives trying to "entice" opposition principals to join their ranks, to "dismantle the opposition." They're aware of these "gimmicks" and "ready for them." Then, he throws shade at Deputy President Kithure Kindiki for allegedly betraying his "brother" by taking his seat after impeachment. This whole speech, delivered in Matapato Ward, Kajiado Central, was while welcoming defectors to his party, signaling their desperate efforts to break into ODM strongholds. A 23-year-old MCA, Abraham Osoi, elected on a Wiper ticket, is now part of this grand plan.

Gachagua, never one to miss an opportunity to complain, also took a jab at President Ruto for his "lack of political goodwill" regarding the Maa community. Roads, a slaughterhouse - all neglected. The Amboseli National Park transition is just a "political decoy." Promises to the Maa community are just for "his own political gain." He wooed them to the opposition, assuring them the Interior security docket would go to one of their own. Meanwhile, DCP Secretary-General John Methu wants to "play fairly" and win in 2027, gaining "political tentacles" and trouncing Kenya Kwanza. Kajiado Senator Samuel Seki echoed the sentiments, claiming the community has been "short-changed" by broken promises.

This whole song and dance is happening in Kajiado Central, a constituency that's been an ODM stronghold for over two decades. Now, with Raila Odinga gone, everyone’s scrambling to make inroads. While Gachagua talks about unity, the reality is a bunch of politicians trying to grab power and attention. They talk about defeating Ruto, but their own internal squabbles and poaching attempts suggest they might be their own worst enemy, just like those who promise posh classrooms but deliver petty crimes. The election is still years away, and this opposition unity feels about as solid as a politician's promise.
