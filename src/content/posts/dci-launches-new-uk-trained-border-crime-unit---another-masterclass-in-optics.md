DCI's Fancy New UK-Trained Border Unit: Another Day, Another Shambles?

So, the Directorate of Criminal Investigations (DCI) has gone and done it again. They've unveiled a brand-new, "specialised" border crime unit. Because, clearly, the existing ones are just too busy polishing their boots to notice anything. This outfit, called the Counter-Organised Immigration Crime Team (COICT) - sounds important, doesn't it? - is apparently the "first of its kind in East Africa." And guess who trained them? The Brits, of course. Because nothing says "we've got this sorted" like foreign consultants.

Twenty-nine officers, all plucked from the ranks and now supposedly ready to tackle human trafficking, smuggling, and all sorts of "transnational organised crime." They’ve had their "intensive training," their "specialised training," all under this fancy Kenya-UK Border Security Programme. Ben Fisher from the British High Commission is calling it a "landmark achievement." Oh, the humanity! Meanwhile, Kenya is still stuck at Tier 2 in the US State Department's Trafficking in Persons Report, meaning they're *trying* but not quite cutting it. What a surprise.

And let's not forget the "frontline border stations." Places like Moyale and Mandera, which are apparently hotbeds for moving migrants to South Africa and the Middle East. Busia and Malaba are linked to child trafficking and forced labour. Just last week, they "rescued" 14 underage Ugandan girls. *Rescued*. Let's hope these new recruits are more effective than the ones who let them get that far in the first place. It makes you wonder if this is just another way for politicians to look like they're doing something, like when the government teams up with France to 'fix' online creeps. [From Fife With Love: Why Is Kenya's National Team Scavenging On Facebook Marketplace?](#) - sometimes the efforts are just for show.

Then there's the little matter of Somalia. Al-Shabaab militants are still doing their thing across the border, attacking communities and security forces. So, while our boys and girls are busy learning how to spot a dodgy passport, the real threats are probably just waltzing in. This whole exercise feels like slapping a fresh coat of paint on a crumbling house. We'll see how long this "landmark achievement" lasts before it’s just another forgotten report gathering dust.
