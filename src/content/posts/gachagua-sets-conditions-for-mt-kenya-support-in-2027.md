Gachagua Demands "Deals" For Mt Kenya Votes In 2027

Rigathi Gachagua, the man who helped Ruto clinch power in 2022, is now saying Mt Kenya won't just hand over its votes like loose change anymore. He's out here demanding "concrete political negotiations" and guarantees, basically saying the region wants a pay cheque for its political loyalty. Forget sentiment; this is about cold, hard political currency.

It seems the lesson from 2022, where they apparently voted "like that... without thinking," has sunk in. Now, Gachagua is telling the opposition bigwigs - Kalonzo, Matiang'i, Karua, you name them - that if they want the Mountain's backing for a united presidential bid in 2027, they'd better bring something tangible to the table. No more blank cheques, just ironclad agreements. It's a clear signal that the days of blind loyalty are over, and this is just another instalment in the ongoing drama of Kenyan politics, where every vote has a price tag.

Gachagua's tough talk isn't just hot air; it's a calculated move to leverage Mt Kenya's voting muscle. This region has always been the kingmaker, and after the 2022 election saw them rally behind a non-native, Gachagua is determined to ensure that influence translates into real political gains, not just symbolic gestures. He's reminding everyone that the Mountain is no longer content with just being a donor to other regions' ambitions.

The former Deputy President is also nudging the lawyers from his backyard to get involved in party primaries. This suggests that internal party wrangling and power plays are just as crucial as the grand opposition alliances. It’s all about controlling the narrative and the machinery from the ground up, ensuring that whatever "deals" are struck, they serve the region's interests, not just the politicians' egos. This whole charade feels like another episode in the endless cycle, reminding me of Ruto's own "No Region Left Behind" tour, which, as we’ve seen, often delivers more promises than actual pavement.
