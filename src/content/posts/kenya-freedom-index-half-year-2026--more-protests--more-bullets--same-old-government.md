THE FREEDOM INDEX HALF YEAR 2026 REPORT: KENYANS STILL SCREAMING INTO THE VOID

So, Odipo Dev and Amnesty International Kenya dropped another report. Big surprise, right? More people are protesting, more live bullets are flying, and more arrests are happening. It’s 2026, and Kenya is still a giant, simmering pot of grievances with the lid about to blow. Apparently, we had 1,292 protest events between January 2025 and June 2026. That's a lot of shouting, and for what? So the fat cats in government can continue ignoring us.

Economic woes are still the main tune on the streets. Last year, it was jobs and pay. This year? Fuel prices are making us all choke on our ugali. Food, rent, school fees - the usual suspects. And who’s getting the blame? The national government, of course. They’re the big target now, with protests nearly doubling in the first half of 2026 compared to all of 2025. It’s the same old song and dance, just with more desperate lyrics and a heavier beat.

The report even has the nerve to talk about "peaceful marches." Honestly, how peaceful can you get when you’re being gassed and shot at? They say the state's response "must not be repression." But that’s precisely what it is, isn’t it? It’s the only language this government seems to understand. We’ve seen hundreds injured and dozens killed, especially around June and July 2025. Remember those days? The independent IPOA and KNCHR have the grim numbers, but does anyone in power actually care? It’s like screaming into the wind. We’re just a bunch of frustrated citizens, and it seems our only option is to keep blocking roads and making noise, hoping someone, somewhere, will eventually listen. It’s a losing game, but what else can we do? It reminds me of when the IEBC tried to stop the TikTok-ification of Kenyan elections; they just want to control the narrative, not fix the problems.

And the weapons of choice? Teargas, followed closely by live ammunition. This isn't crowd control; this is state-sanctioned violence. The report highlights how lethal force is only supposed to be used when lives are in imminent danger, not to disperse a protest. But here we are, with police treating legitimate dissent as a security threat. It's all documented, of course, in media reports and YouTube videos, meticulously collected by these organisations. But statistics don't bleed, and they don't die. They just sit there, a testament to how little things have changed, how much hot air is being blown while people suffer.
