So, President Ruto is busy launching "Vision 2060" at KICC, promising us a Singapore-esque Kenya. Great. Just what we need - more lofty dreams from the same old people who've been selling us snake oil for decades. While he's up there talking about fiscal discipline and slaying dragons of graft, down here, his own party is doing a spectacular impression of a sinking Titanic. Mt Kenya leaders are jumping ship faster than you can say "election rigging."

The latest to bail is Gatundu South's Gabriel Kagombe, moaning about unfulfilled pledges like Mau Mau road repairs and farmer promises. Honestly, who are these people kidding? They’re all the same, jumping to wherever the wind - or the cash - is blowing. Kiambu Governor Kimani Wamatangi is hinting he’s out, Murang'a's Irungu Kang'ata and Nyandarua's Kiarie Badilisha have already waved goodbye to UDA. Lamu Woman Rep Muthoni Marubu has also severed ties. Looks like Rigathi Gachagua's Democracy for the Citizens Party is shaping up to be the main beneficiary of this political exodus.

And while all this political musical chairs is happening, let's not forget the real cost of our so-called democracy. The Kenya Freedom Index is out, and it's not pretty. Between 2020 and 2025, over 138 people killed, 1,227 injured, and 2,128 arrested during protests. The youth and ordinary folks are bearing the brunt, paying with their lives for demanding change. 2025 was a bloodbath, with 70 dead, easily surpassing the deaths during the Gen Z protests. It just goes to show, when the people scream, the response is often bullets and batons, not solutions. It’s a stark reminder of the human cost of citizen action, a theme we've seen play out before, like in the tragic events surrounding Dr. Mutiso's death.

Meanwhile, Siaya Governor James Orengo is out here claiming there are plans to sabotage a rally, even alleging that weapons like rungus and arrows were distributed after a UDA-ODM meeting. Seriously? Are we going back to the Stone Age? It seems the political games are getting dirtier, and the stakes are getting higher, with ordinary Kenyans still caught in the crossfire. This whole Vision 2060 thing feels like a distraction while the real show - the scramble for power and the inevitable disappointments - is happening right before our eyes.
