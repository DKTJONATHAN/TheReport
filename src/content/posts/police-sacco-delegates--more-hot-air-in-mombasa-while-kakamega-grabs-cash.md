Police Sacco Delegates: More Hot Air in Mombasa While Kakamega Grabs Cash

WARNING: Another lavish police gathering, this time in Mombasa, while ordinary Kenyans in Kakamega get a paltry Sh5m. Seems some delegates are more interested in fancy resorts than real member service.

The Kenya National Police Sacco has decided Mombasa's swanky Continental Resort is the perfect place for their Cohort Three Delegates Workshop. Because nothing says "leadership development" like overpriced cocktails and talking shops, right? Expect a lot of handshakes and platitudes from the leadership and management, all while the ordinary members probably wonder where their dues are actually going.

Retired Deputy Inspector General Edward Mbugua graced the occasion as chief guest. One can only imagine the tales of "service" he regaled them with. Meanwhile, the Board of Directors, led by National Chairman David Sohelo Mategwa, OGW, HSC, DSA, ICUDE - quite the alphabet soup of titles - are surely deepening their understanding of how to keep the gravy train rolling.

While these delegates are busy "exchanging experiences" and "deepening their understanding," news from Kakamega's Butere groups offers a stark contrast. They're receiving Sh5 million in social grants. So, while the police bigwigs are enjoying the coastal breeze, actual communities are getting a bit of tangible help. It makes you wonder if these workshops are about empowering members or just lining pockets with more jargon and lukewarm coffee.

This whole " Cohort Three workshop" is apparently part of a "broader push" to equip leaders. Equip them with what, exactly? The ability to write more press releases like this one? Or perhaps the skills to navigate the labyrinth of bureaucracy to secure more funds for these elaborate retreats? It’s a familiar song and dance, a spectacle of supposed progress that often leaves the average citizen wondering if any of it actually trickles down. It’s a bit like that time a zealous Ruto campaigner got caught up in a fuel import scandal; the big players always seem to be a step ahead, or at least well-fed.

The Sacco review team is keen to hear from you, no doubt expecting more glowing reports. But for those of us watching from the outside, it looks like another exercise in self-congratulation. For comments and clarifications, you know where to write. Just don't expect any real change, unless it's another venue booked for the next "essential" gathering.
