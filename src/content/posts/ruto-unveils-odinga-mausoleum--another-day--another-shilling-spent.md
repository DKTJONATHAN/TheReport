So, the President, His Excellency Dr. William Samoei Ruto, has officially opened the Raila Amolo Odinga Mausoleum in Bondo. Apparently, this is a "testament to Kenya’s rich national heritage" and the "enduring legacy" of Uncle Raila. The KDF, the very people meant to be defending us, were roped in to build this thing in a "record two months". Sounds efficient, right? Just not sure if "efficiency" is the word I'd use when our roads are potholes and our hospitals are understaffed.

They say the Ministry of Defence took over the project in July 2026. So, not only are our soldiers building memorials, but they're doing it with government funds that could have gone somewhere, anywhere, else. The KDF even mounted a Guard of Honour. For a tomb. Because, you know, that's a top national security priority. Meanwhile, some of us are still trying to figure out how to afford basic necessities. It’s like watching The Illusion of Wealth: How Forex Influencers Manipulate Nairobi’s Digital Economy play out in real life, but with taxpayer money.

The Odinga family is apparently grateful, especially to the KDF. "Dedication, discipline and commitment," they gushed. Yes, dedication to following orders, I suppose. And the family, bless their hearts, is inviting everyone to visit, "regardless of their views". Well, that's magnanimous. Maybe they can invite the folks struggling to pay their rent next door.

The President, predictably, "accorded due recognition" to the construction team. Because, of course, he would. It’s all about optics, isn’t it? Building a monument to a political figure, with the military. It screams stability and national unity, doesn't it? Or maybe it just screams that some legacies are more important than others, and that the military is just another handy tool for whatever project the powers-that-be decide on.

Ultimately, this mausoleum is being presented as KDF preserving "Kenya’s national legacy and heritage". But what about the living heritage? The people who need schools, healthcare, and jobs? This is a very expensive marble tombstone, built by men who should be protecting borders, for a man who’s already gone. And we’re supposed to see this as a win.
