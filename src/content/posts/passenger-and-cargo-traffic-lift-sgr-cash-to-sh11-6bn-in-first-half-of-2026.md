SGR Cash Pile Grows, But Don't Get Excited

**WARNING:** The Standard Gauge Railway is making more money, but a closer look reveals the usual Kenyan economic shenanigans. Don't expect this cash to fix anything.









So, the SGR managed to squeeze Sh11.6 billion out of us in the first half of 2026. Passenger revenue is up, supposedly because more people are crammed into their trains. Fancy that, schools close, kids travel, and suddenly the numbers look good. It’s always the same predictable rhythm, isn't it? Makes you wonder if those holiday periods are just conveniently timed to pad the stats.

Then there's the freight. More cargo moved, sure, but the revenue growth is a joke. They’re moving mountains of goods, 15.1 percent more tonnes, but the cash is barely inching up. Apparently, the "mix of cargo, rates or the routes used" messed things up. Translation: somebody’s cutting deals, playing games with pricing, or most likely, the real profits are being siphoned off elsewhere. It’s the classic Nairobi hustle, just on a bigger scale.

The numbers for April are a perfect example. More cargo, but less money than the year before. How does that even happen? It’s like they’re deliberately trying to confuse us, or more likely, they're just not being honest about where the real earnings are. We saw a similar charade with how Felista Mugo’s fights were hyped - a lot of noise, but is it really changing anything on the ground? This SGR story feels like more of the same.

And let's not forget the February anomaly. Massive revenue jump on tonnes that barely moved much. It screams "creative accounting" louder than a herd of wildebeest crossing the Mara. While the SGR lines their pockets, think about the billions being "thrown at a fancy tomb" for Raila Odinga - a prime example of where our money *isn't* going. This Sh11.6 billion is just another statistic to distract us from the rot.
