The math of the coming collapse is cold and unforgiving. Research suggests that the safe-haven status of the dollar is more fragile than the hubris of Washington allows. If the global demand for dollar-denominated assets evaporates, the United States will find itself in the same gutter as every other profligate empire in history. We are looking at a permanent real depreciation of nearly nine percent. This is not just a statistical adjustment; it is a direct tax on every American consumer who has grown used to cheap foreign goods subsidized by the rest of the world's labor.

The ripple effects will be felt far beyond the shopping malls of the West. When the convenience yield vanishes, US interest rates will spike by at least 90 basis points. In a nation where public debt is already 100 percent of GDP, this is a fiscal death sentence. The US government will be forced to choose between hyper-inflation and a total dismantling of its remaining social safety net. The 33-trillion-dollar present value of seigniorage that the US currently enjoys is essentially a ghost asset. Once the world stops believing in the ghost, the wealth disappears into thin air, taking the global financial architecture with it.

International markets are already seeing the cracks. The weaponization of the dollar through sanctions has proven that safety is a relative term. If your assets can be frozen at the whim of a mid-level bureaucrat in D.C., they are no longer safe; they are hostages. This realization is driving the shift toward BRICS-led systems that bypass the dollar entirely. The BIS data showing 18-trillion-dollars in offshore dollar assets is not a sign of strength, but a measure of the systemic risk that will eventually ignite. When the "collateral backbone" of the world snaps, the resulting liquidity black hole will swallow emerging markets and developed nations alike.

While local institutions continue to peddle optimistic narratives, such as the hollow promises found at the [Moi Forces Academy Career Day](/posts/moi-forces-academy-career-day--more-fancy-talk-than-future-fortune), the global reality is far more predatory. The transition from a unipolar dollar world to a fragmented one will be violent and chaotic. There is no soft landing when you have spent decades jumping from a skyscraper and convincing yourself you are flying. The exorbitant privilege is ending, and the bill is coming due with interest that the world can no longer afford to pay.
