So, the National Police Service, in their infinite wisdom, finally decided to pipe up about a fake document circulating social media claiming new Officers Commanding Stations (OCSs) for Nairobi. It’s like they’re surprised people are dumb enough to believe anything they see online, especially when it involves positions of power. This fabricated list, complete with "appointments" for over 30 stations, must have given some ambitious characters a brief moment of false hope.

Of course, NPS, in their typically uninspired fashion, called it "fake" and "unverified." Shocker. They’re reminding us that real appointments come through "official statements and authorised channels." As if most of us are holding our breath waiting for a stamped memo from Jogoo House. It’s almost funny, if it wasn’t for the fact that this is the same police service that’s supposed to be keeping our streets safe, a task they seem to struggle with more than a drunkard on a unicycle.

This whole charade is happening amidst the usual pre-election jitters, with crime figures supposedly on the rise. You know, the same "concerns" that pop up every election cycle. It’s almost as predictable as a Ruto lead in the polls, even with a fifth of voters still clueless about who to back. Perhaps this fake list was just a distraction from the fact that they've also been busy restructuring, creating new "specialised formations" like the Nairobi Metropolitan Police. Because what Nairobi really needs is more bureaucracy, not more boots on the ground actually doing something.

They’ve rolled out a whole new organisational framework, adding units like the Marine Unit and Quick Response Unit to the existing cast of characters like the GSU and Traffic Police. All this restructuring, all these new units, and yet the crime rate seems to have a life of its own. It’s a classic case of rearranging the deck chairs on the Titanic, while the real problem-whether it’s actual crime or just another manufactured crisis for profit-continues to sink us. At least Kamukunji Police Station is slated to become the headquarters, complete with an "Integrated Command and Communication Centre." Let's hope it actually integrates something useful, unlike the endless stream of misinformation we’re already drowning in.
