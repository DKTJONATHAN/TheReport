ODM's "Demands" for Ruto Ahead of Their Big Naivasha "Bonding" Session









So, the big wigs - Ruto and Oburu Oginga, no less - are trotting off to Naivasha for a three-day "retreat." Apparently, they need to "strengthen their working relationship" and "prepare for 2027." Translation: they're going to eat, drink, and strategize how to keep us all exactly where we are, just with slightly shinier campaign promises. The agenda? "Working arrangement," a "10-point agenda" that sounds suspiciously like last year's wishlist, and, of course, the grand prize: 2027. It's all about survival, folks, not solving anything.

Gladys Wanga, bless her heart, says they'll update each other on the "ODM-UDA partnership" and review progress on "issues arising from Nadco." That’s code for "did we get what we wanted?" and "how can we spin this so it looks like we did something?" They'll be dissecting everything from "grassroots mobilisation" (aka, how to convince Mama Mboga to vote for them again) to "political realignments" (aka, who's selling out who this week). Remember when all this started? It was all about the "broad-based government" and a "10-point agenda" hammered out with the late Raila. Now it's just another political circus.

And speaking of realignments, the ground seems to be shifting under Ruto’s feet, especially in Mt Kenya. Some of his 2022 star players are looking for new digs, which means all hands are on deck to keep those votes flowing. They're also apparently worried about Edwin Sifuna's "Linda Mwananchi" movement stealing ODM's thunder in their own backyard. It’s a classic Nairobi story, isn't it? Everyone scrambling for a slice of the pie, convinced they're the only ones who can deliver the goods. You'd think after all this time, we'd learn. It's like trying to solve Nairobi's traffic jams with more matatus - the [Mzungu Dream is Just a Low-Budget Horror Movie in Nairobi](/posts/the-mzungu-dream-is-just-a-low-budget-horror-movie-in-nairobi).

Then there's the running mate drama brewing. UDA and ODM are already tripping over each other trying to bag that coveted spot next to Ruto. Some ODM folks are already pushing for Wycliffe Oparanya, which is apparently causing headaches for Kithure Kindiki's crew. Mombasa Governor Abdullswamad Nassir tried to downplay it, saying it’s too many people for such sensitive talks, and it’ll be handled by a "technical committee." Basically, they’re kicking the can down the road until it’s politically convenient. "Bonding," he calls it. I call it a glorified strategy meeting where they’ll figure out how to divide the spoils.

The whole "political zoning" thing is another can of worms they'll probably swat away with vague promises. ODM wants their turf protected, while UDA wants the freedom to throw their hat in every ring. Junet Mohammed is talking about a "roadmap" for 2027, which sounds like a fancy way of saying "here’s the plan to keep us in power." Meanwhile, Kimilili MP Didmus Barasa is focused on "messaging" and listening to constituent complaints so they can "handle" them - which usually means a press release and a photo op. Don't expect any real solutions, just more political theatre.
