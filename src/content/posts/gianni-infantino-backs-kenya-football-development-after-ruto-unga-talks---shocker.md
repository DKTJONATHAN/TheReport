So, Gianni Infantino, the big boss at FIFA, met with our very own President Ruto at the UNGA. And what do you know, it was "productive." Apparently, Ruto is now a champion for football development. Because that's exactly what Kenya needs right now, isn't it? More talk about sports while the actual country is falling apart.

Infantino’s all smiles, recalling their "previous engagement" and President’s "support." Support for what, exactly? Building more stadiums for kids who can't even get to school safely because the roads are a death trap? We're talking about Chwele traders calling for action on accident-prone roads in Bungoma County, but here we are, hearing about how Kenya’s football future is brighter than ever.

It’s like watching them [Selling the Kitchen to Buy a Meal: The KPC Fire Sale](/posts/selling-the-kitchen-to-buy-a-meal--the-kpc-fire-sale) all over again. They focus on the shiny, feel-good stories while the real problems fester. Infantino’s endorsement is just another tick on the "progress" checklist, a photo op that distracts from the daily grind of survival for most Kenyans.

This whole "backing football development" narrative feels as hollow as a politician’s promise during election season. We've seen this play before. Lots of fanfare, high-level meetings, and then… crickets. Meanwhile, the infrastructure crumbles, and the average mwananchi is left to navigate a reality that’s far less glamorous than a FIFA press conference.

The Eastleigh Voice, bless their optimistic hearts, reports this like it’s actual news. A productive meeting. Support for football. It’s the same old song and dance. Let's see how much of this "support" actually trickles down to the dusty fields and the broken-down buses. My money's on very little.

So, while Infantino flies back to his plush offices and Ruto poses for more pictures, remember the Chwele traders. Remember the potholes. Remember that talk is cheap, especially when it comes from international bodies and politicians. This "backing" is just another puff of smoke.
