The upcoming MPC meeting is just another exercise in waiting for the inevitable. Inflation is not a domestic choice for India; it is a global export. With oil prices acting as a constant sword of Damocles over the rupee, any reassessment of growth-inflation dynamics is merely an admission that the central bank is reactive, not proactive. They are slaves to the barrel price and the whims of foreign energy cartels.

Much like the misplaced priorities seen in the Solai settlement saga (/posts/the-solai-settlement-saga--why-kenyan-influencers-are-trading-brain-cells-for-clout), where influencers traded brain cells for clout, central bankers trade long-term structural integrity for short-term market optics. They pretend the liquidity will naturally withdraw, ignoring the fact that capital flight is often the only natural outcome when emerging markets face sustained external shocks. The international community should see this for what it is: a fragile hold-fast against a rising tide of global economic instability that India cannot contain.
