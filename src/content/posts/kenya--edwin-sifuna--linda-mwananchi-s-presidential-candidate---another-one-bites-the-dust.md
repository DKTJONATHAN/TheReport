So, Linda Mwananchi has decided Edwin Sifuna is their guy for the 2027 presidential race. Apparently, he's got a knack for rattling President Ruto's cage, which, let's be honest, is a popular pastime around here. Younger folks are supposedly flocking to him. Fascinating. We've seen this movie before, haven't we?

The whole song and dance of "trusted, independent news" comes with a catch, naturally. They want you to swallow their cookies - personal data, IP addresses, browsing habits - all in the name of "improving your experience." If you're not careful, you'll end up with restricted access. It’s the same old story, whether it’s about politicians or news sites wanting to mine you for data. They offer you a "free" path, but it costs you your privacy.

Of course, there's always the "subscribe for full access" option. For 1 Euro, you get "unlimited access" and no "advertising cookies." But then they hit you with the fine print: they and their 77 partners still want to process your personal data for a laundry list of purposes, from personalized advertising to precise geolocation. It's a clever way to get you to pay and still have their hands in your digital pocket. Just another reminder of how things work in this country, and frankly, everywhere else.

Sifuna is being paraded as Ruto’s challenger. Ten things to know about him, they say. Ten things that will probably amount to the same old political platitudes. Remember when Kenya Power plunged Nyamira into darkness? [Blackout or Just Another Excuse?](/posts/kenya-power-plunges-nyamira-into-darkness-march-15--blackout-or-just-another-excuse). It’s a similar kind of narrative: problems arise, explanations are offered, and eventually, things just… continue. This presidential bid is likely to be another chapter in that same predictable book.
